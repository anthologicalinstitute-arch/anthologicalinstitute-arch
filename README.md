# Anthological Institute for Research & Governance

**Advanced Analytics · Mathematical Modeling · Evidence Quality · AI Governance**

The Anthological Institute for Research & Governance develops structured research methods and analytical frameworks to support evidence-informed decisions, transparent reasoning, and responsible technology governance.

## Research Areas

- **Advanced Analytics** — Quantitative analysis, data interpretation, and decision support.
- **Mathematical Modeling** — Statistical methods, simulation, sensitivity analysis, and uncertainty assessment.
- **Evidence Quality** — Source reliability, freshness, corroboration, provenance, and completeness.
- **AI Research Governance** — Model evaluation, transparency, auditability, and human oversight.
- **Research Documentation** — Reproducible methods, explicit assumptions, and traceable findings.

## Research Principles

1. Distinguish evidence from interpretation and hypothesis.
2. Document sources, dates, assumptions, and limitations.
3. Evaluate uncertainty and avoid unsupported certainty.
4. Favor reproducible analysis and transparent methodology.
5. Identify exploratory frameworks as unvalidated until appropriate testing supports stronger claims.

## Research Program

Our research program focuses on evidence-quality methods, quantitative analysis, mathematical modeling, and responsible AI governance.

Research repositories will document methodologies, supporting sources, examples, validation status, and known limitations.

## Production Completeness Checklist

A repository is listed as production-complete only when all items are met:

- [ ] Public visibility confirmed
- [ ] README states purpose, scope, and intended users
- [ ] Methods, data sources, and dates documented
- [ ] Assumptions and known limitations documented
- [ ] Validation status stated (validated, partially validated, or unvalidated)
- [ ] Reproducible: instructions or code sufficient to repeat results
- [ ] License included
- [ ] Reviewed and approved by the research owner
- [ ] Release tag or version recorded

Exploratory frameworks, including those using Quantum Sims™, are identified as unvalidated until appropriate testing supports stronger claims.
